01/ABOUT

Two sides.
One sound.

DAY//NIGHT is a Romanian producer duo formed by Midnight and XERØ, two artists brought together by a shared vision of pushing boundaries in modern electronic music.

Although officially emerging as a project in 2024, the foundation of DAY//NIGHT was built through years of creative collaboration on multiple productions and experimental concepts developed together.

Known for blending powerful EDM energy with a wide spectrum of genres, DAY//NIGHT refuses to be limited by a single sound. Their music travels seamlessly between Dubstep, Reggaeton, Latin-inspired rhythms, Commercial Dance, Festival EDM, and crossover electronic productions designed for both clubs and massive live experiences.

Before establishing DAY//NIGHT, Midnight and XERØ collaborated on several independent projects, including the innovative EDManele concept, while also contributing to releases distributed through major Romanian labels such as Cat Music and Global Records. These experiences helped shape their signature style: unpredictable, high-energy, and emotionally driven.

Driven by the idea that music should connect worlds, DAY//NIGHT represents the contrast between light and darkness, chaos and balance, melody and power, transforming different influences into a unique sonic identity.

With a constantly evolving sound and an ambition that reaches far beyond borders, DAY//NIGHT is not here to follow trends. They are here to create the future of electronic music.

From Romania to the world.

This is DAY//NIGHT.
