PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

When you contact DAY//NIGHT, we use the details you provide only to respond to your inquiry.

Data controller

DAY//NIGHT™ Group manages inquiries submitted through this website. Privacy requests can be sent to management@daynightgroup.ro.

Information collected

We collect the name, email address, subject and message that you choose to submit. Anti-spam services may also process limited technical information required to protect the form.

Purpose and legal basis

The information is used only to respond to booking, collaboration and business inquiries and to protect the website from abuse. We do not sell form submissions.

Processors

Cloudflare may process the request and anti-spam verification. An email delivery provider may process the message so it can reach DAY//NIGHT management.

Retention

Inquiry data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer and manage the request, unless a longer period is required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of information related to your inquiry by contacting management@daynightgroup.ro.
